# VANGUARD-03

## Workflow identity

> VANGUARD-03: unmanned Marvel-inspired exploration probe about two metres across, tangible scientific machine rather than superhero costume. Low hexagonal deep-crimson body, flat graphite deck, satin-gold inset side panels. NO wheels. Three identical downward pulse-thruster housings form a balanced triangle firmly integrated beneath the body; fictional pulse lift enables low hover. Three short hinged landing legs attach around the lower rim with broad footpads clear of the outlets. One short central camera mast carries a dark lens block. One compact two-joint sampling arm attaches to a distinct FRONT panel, folded against it for travel; opposite rear panel plain. Matte red paint, dark heat-resistant nozzle rims, lightly brushed gold panels and tidy recessed fasteners. No cockpit, floating components, giant energy core or weapons.

## Scenes

### 01 — Hello world

Immediately after arrival at a shallow crater, VANGUARD hovers half a metre above the ground in full front-left view. Landing legs folded clear of outlets, arm folded. Only restrained pale glow inside dark thruster rims, no long flames. Low irregular dust veil spreads close to the surface; a separate shadow falls below and slightly right of the probe. New crimson and satin-gold panels carry a few dust grains. Small carrier platform distant near crater rim. Early side sunlight, textured stony foreground, empty dusty sky. No wheel tracks or superhero effects.

### 02 — Rock investigation

VANGUARD has landed beside a fractured dark rock inside a shallow crater. Front-right photograph at body height showing the entire probe, rock and support contacts. Three landing legs extended, broad footpads firmly planted. Pulse thrusters completely OFF: dark outlets, no airborne dust. One front sampling arm reaches from its mount through two connected joints to touch a fresh fracture. Small pebbles around footpads, little pale powder at the contact. Graphite deck, crimson shell and gold panels have subdued natural reflections and fine settled dust. Clear late-morning light. No wheels, additional arms or floating instruments.

[View image](scenes/02-rock-investigation.png)

### 03 — Three years later

VANGUARD after three Earth years, hovering low across a weathered crater floor. Rear-right photograph with layered crater rim distant. Original hexagonal crimson body and gold panels recognizable but dulled by uneven dust and small abrasions. Thicker dust in protected seams, subtle wear on leading edges, local heat discoloration on dark nozzle rims without dramatic soot. Arm folded on far front panel; legs folded clear of three working thrusters. Restrained ground-hugging dust disturbance and separated shadow establish hover height. Muted sunlight through atmospheric dust, scattered rocks, no wheel tracks. Worn but maintained, not abandoned.
