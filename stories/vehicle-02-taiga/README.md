# TAIGA-02

## Workflow identity

> TAIGA-02: roughly two-metre-long unmanned Russian planetary-exploration-inspired Mars rover. Squat cylindrical brushed-silver instrument housing with a closed flat top on a rectangular dark olive-green chassis. Four broad solid-metal wheels, two per side, with raised chevron ribs and dark hubs. Each wheel connects visibly to a sturdy short suspension arm. A short camera post sits at the FRONT edge of the circular housing with one dark lens block. One robust two-joint drilling arm mounts at the FRONT centre of the chassis, ending in a short cylindrical drill head, folded against the front for travel. Rear is a plain olive service panel. Restrained housing seams, recessed fasteners, matte green paint and enclosed dark joints. Scientific field instrument, no weaponry, cabin or ornamental antennas.

## Scenes

### 01 — Hello world

First survey after touchdown near an eroded basin. TAIGA fully visible from front-right on flat gravel beside shallow wind-shaped ridges. Its squat descent platform is small in the distance, a short pair of ribbed-wheel tracks leading toward the rover. Early sunlight reflects softly across the circular silver housing; green chassis and four ribbed wheels remain readable in open shadow. A little landing dust rests on the lower chassis, otherwise new surfaces. Drill folded, camera upright. Layered outcrops beyond the plain, no fog or excessive exhaust haze.

### 02 — Rock investigation

TAIGA inspects a low sedimentary outcrop at the foot of a dry basin slope. Front-left photograph showing the full rover and a metre-scale exposed ledge. The single drilling arm extends from its front mount through two solid joints, short drill head touching a pale horizontal rock band. A compact mound of fresh powder lies below the shallow drill point. Four wheels rest on stable gravel with plausible axle alignment. Fine dust on the silver housing, subdued metal reflections. Side light reveals thin irregular rock layers, fractures and raised wheel ribs. No sparks, giant drill rig, loose cables or extra tools.

[View image](scenes/02-rock-investigation.png)

### 03 — Three years later

TAIGA after three Earth years, still operating on a broad dry valley floor. Rear-right photograph preserves its circular silver housing and square green undercarriage, front drill folded on the far side. Red dust fills housing seams and wheel ribs. Olive paint is faded and locally scuffed on lower edges; silver surfaces dulled by abrasion, not mirror polished. Suspension and camera intact. A recent wheel trail curves between scattered stones. Low stratified escarpment recedes into a dusty horizon. Soft available afternoon light and ordinary expedition-camera framing, enduring scientific machine rather than cinematic wreck.
